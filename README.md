PREDICTING DELIVERY PERFORMANCE

Chosen problem: Delivery performance. Two framings of the same underlying outcome are developed side by
side, off the same data preparation and the same prediction point.
The recurring decision belongs to Olist's Operations team: at the moment an order is placed, should this order
be routed through standard fulfilment, or flagged for proactive handling – prioritised warehouse pick-and-pack,
an upgraded carrier lane, or an early, honest notification to the customer that their parcel is at risk of arriving
late? Today, every order receives identical treatment regardless of its risk profile, and the only signal shown to
the customer is a single estimated delivery date generated by the platform at checkout. That date is treated in
this report as the current, non-personalised practice against which any model must be judged.
